## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C. Finely chop the onion and garlic. Finely chop the parsley. Grease a baking dish large enough to hold the 2 salmon fillets in a single layer.
2. Heat the mild olive oil in a sauté pan. Add the anchovies with 0.5 tbsp of their oil, the onion and the chilli flakes. Cook gently over low heat for 3 minutes. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute more. Stir in the tomato pulp, all the olives and capers, and the parsley. Bring to the boil, then simmer over medium-low heat for about 10 minutes to reduce the sauce.
3. Pour half the sauce into the baking dish, arrange the salmon fillets on top, and pour over the remaining sauce. Bake in the centre of the oven for about 15 minutes, until just cooked. The handwritten note gives a core temperature of 60°C.
4. Remove the salmon from the oven, drizzle with the extra-virgin olive oil, and serve with baguette.

## Notes

- Scaled from 4 servings to 2 by halving all specified ingredient quantities. Baguette is to serve; the source does not specify an amount.
- Follows the confirmed handwritten adaptations: 200°C, about 15 minutes in the oven, a 60°C core-temperature note, parsley in the sauce, and no spinach. All the olives and capers go into the sauce; the separate garnish is omitted. The oil used to cook the spinach is also omitted.
