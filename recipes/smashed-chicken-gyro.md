## Instructions

1. Make the tzatziki. Squeeze the excess liquid from the grated cucumber. Mix with the Greek yoghurt, garlic, lemon juice, dill, mint, a pinch of salt and cracked black pepper.
2. Spread the raw chicken mince across one side of the pita in a thin, even layer, pressing all the way to the edges. Season with Greek seasoning, salt and pepper, then spray lightly with olive oil.
3. Heat a frying pan over medium-high heat. Place the pita chicken-side down and press firmly with a spatula for even contact. Cook for about 4–5 minutes until golden and the chicken is cooked through (74°C in the centre). Flip and cook for another 2–3 minutes until the pita is crisp.
4. Spoon the tzatziki over the chicken. Add shredded lettuce, sliced tomato and red onion. Squeeze over the lemon wedge, fold and serve while crisp.
